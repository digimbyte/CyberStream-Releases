# ◆ CyberStream Player

### Your music. Your queue. Your stream.

A Windows music player made with Twitch and OBS in mind — and just as at home playing music on its own.

**Twitch and OBS are entirely optional. No stream or Twitch account is needed to listen.**

[**Download for Windows**](https://github.com/digimbyte/CyberStream-Releases/releases/latest) · [What's new](https://github.com/digimbyte/CyberStream-Releases/releases)

</div>

## 🎧 Press play. Make it yours.

Build a soundtrack for your stream, a gaming session, or an ordinary afternoon. Add music to your library, choose what plays, and let the queue keep things moving.

- **Bring your music together.** Add local music files and folders, or supported music links from YouTube, SoundCloud, and Spotify.
- **Keep your files where they belong.** Your local music stays in its original folders.
- **Choose your rotation.** Enable the library items you want in the mix and switch others off without removing them.
- **See what's coming.** Follow the current track and upcoming queue, or shuffle things up.
- **Take the controls.** Play, pause, stop, skip, seek within a track, and adjust the volume.

Spotify links help you find music to play; the audio may be a matching version from YouTube.

## 💬 Let chat join the soundtrack

Link Twitch when you want viewers to make requests. Chat can submit a music link using your chosen request command, and you manage those requests from the player.

You decide who can request music, whether to review requests or use automatic acceptance, and how accepted requests are kept: just for the session, saved to the library but switched off, or saved for future playback.

Accept a request, let it join the queue, and keep control of what plays next. Twitch adds audience participation to the player; you can leave it disconnected and use your own library instead.

## ✨ Give your stream a soundtrack

CyberStream is designed to sit alongside OBS, with a now-playing overlay that brings the current track and artwork into your scene. Customize its appearance to suit your stream using the player's overlay style controls.

Separate volume controls let you balance listening and streaming:

| Control | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Local** | The music you hear on your computer. |
| **Stream** | The music sent through the OBS overlay. |
| **Master** | Both volumes together. |

Use the **Streamer Overlay** link in the player to add it to OBS. If you're only listening, simply open CyberStream and press play — OBS never needs to be running.

## 🚀 Start listening

1. [Download the latest release](https://github.com/digimbyte/CyberStream-Releases/releases/latest) and choose **CyberStreamPlayer.exe**.
2. Keep it in a folder of your choice and open it.
3. Open **Library** and add your music.
4. Choose something to play. Connect Twitch or add the OBS overlay whenever you want those extras.

## ↗ Stay up to date

CyberStream checks for updates when it opens. When a new version is available, choose **Update and restart**, or **Later** to keep listening. Your library and settings stay with you.
